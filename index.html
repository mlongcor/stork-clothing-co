Our story & process

How Stork Works

We believe new parents deserve one less thing to worry about. Here's exactly how we make that happen.

Step by step

Your Stork Journey

1

Create Your Baby's Profile

Tell us your baby's name, date of birth, and current clothing size. We also ask about fabric preferences (we love organic cotton!) and any style notes — whether you're into classic neutrals, bold prints, or playful patterns.

2

Choose Your Plan

Pick from our Starter (5 pieces), Grow (8 pieces), or Luxe (10 pieces) monthly subscription plans. All plans include free shipping and free returns. You can upgrade, downgrade, or pause anytime.

3

Our Stylists Hand-Pick Your Box

Our in-house baby stylists curate a coordinated set of clothing pieces tailored to your profile. Every item is certified GOTS organic, OEKO-TEX safe, and machine washable. No harsh dyes, no synthetic materials near sensitive skin.

4

Delivery, Right on Schedule

Your box ships on the same date each month. We proactively size up as your baby grows — you'll never receive clothes that are already too small. Track your box in real time with our shipping notifications.

5

Keep Everything You Love

Keep the whole box and you're charged nothing extra. Not in love with a piece? Our free returns are postage-paid and hassle-free. Your feedback shapes next month's curation — the longer you subscribe, the better your boxes get.

6

Grow With Us Beyond 12 Months

As your baby approaches 12 months, we'll invite you to transition to our Toddler plan (coming soon!) so the magic never stops. Seamless, automatic, and tailored all the way.

What we stand for

Our Values

🌱

Sustainably Made

Every piece is GOTS-certified organic cotton. We partner only with factories audited for fair labour practices and environmental responsibility.

🧴

Skin-Safe Always

OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified. No azo dyes, no formaldehyde, no harmful chemicals — ever. We test every batch before it goes in a box.

♻️

Less Waste

Our packaging is 100% recycled and recyclable. We accept clothes back for our Stork Resale program, keeping baby fashion in circulation longer.

💛

Parent-First

Built by parents, for parents. Every feature, policy, and design decision starts with one question: does this make a new parent's life easier?

Born from a sleepless night

Stork Clothing Co was started in 2023 by two first-time parents who were exhausted, overwhelmed, and staring at an empty dresser at 2am — three days before their baby was due to size out of newborn clothes.

We thought: why isn't there a service that just… handles this? So we built it. Starting with a simple promise — the right clothes, the right size, at the right time — and growing it into something thousands of families now rely on every month.

We're a small, passionate team headquartered in Austin, TX. Every box is packed with intention, and every customer is treated like family.
